# CASCADE Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 12 November 2026**

CASCADE is an offline-first puzzle game. This policy explains what the app stores and how it is used.

## The short version

CASCADE does not require an account. The app does not include advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking, a leaderboard service, or a game backend. Your gameplay data is stored locally on your device and is not sent to the developer.

## Information stored on your device

To provide the game and remember your progress, CASCADE stores the following information locally using the device's app storage:

- Your high score and game statistics.
- Your daily challenge results.
- Your unfinished game, so you can resume it.
- Your sound and haptic preferences.

This information is not uploaded to the developer. Local data may be included in device backups according to your operating-system settings.

## Permissions and device features

CASCADE can play its built-in sound effects and use the device's haptic feedback when those settings are enabled. The game does not require access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

## Advertising, analytics and tracking

CASCADE does not display ads, use third-party advertising SDKs, perform analytics, or track you across apps and websites.

## Third-party services

CASCADE is built using Expo and React Native. Apple, your device operating system, and app distribution services may process information under their own privacy policies when you download or use apps. CASCADE does not send your gameplay data to those services.

## Your choices and deleting data

You can disable sound and haptic feedback in the game's Settings. To remove the locally stored game data, delete CASCADE from your device. Device backups and their retention are controlled by your operating-system settings.

## Children and families

CASCADE does not require registration and has no advertising or social features. Parents and guardians can use the family and privacy controls provided by the device platform.

## Changes to this policy

If CASCADE's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant app release. The effective date above will also be updated.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the [CASCADE GitHub repository](https://github.com/asem909463-oss/CASCADE).
