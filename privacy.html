Privacy, plainly.

Effective date: 12 November 2026
The short version
CASCADE works offline. It does not require an account and does not send your scores or settings to the developer. The app contains no ads, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Information stored on your device

CASCADE stores your high score, game statistics, daily challenge results, unfinished game, and sound and haptic preferences locally on your device so your progress can be remembered. This information is not uploaded to the developer. Local data may be included in device backups according to your operating-system settings.

Permissions and device features

CASCADE can play built-in sound effects and use device haptic feedback when enabled. It does not require access to contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

CASCADE does not display ads, use third-party advertising SDKs, perform analytics, or track you across apps and websites.

Third-party services

CASCADE is built using Expo and React Native. Apple, your device operating system, and app distribution services may process information under their own privacy policies when you download or use apps. CASCADE does not send your gameplay data to those services.

Your choices and deleting data

You can disable sound and haptic feedback in the game's Settings. To remove locally stored game data, delete CASCADE from your device. Device backups and their retention are controlled by your operating-system settings.

Children and families

CASCADE does not require registration and has no advertising or social features. Parents and guardians can use the family and privacy controls provided by the device platform.

Changes to this policy

If CASCADE's data practices change, this policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant app release. The effective date above will also be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the CASCADE GitHub repository.
